# Intro recordings

The language switch in the nav has a play button for each language once its
recording exists. About thirty seconds each, read naturally; change any
wording that doesn't sound like you.

To add one: record it (a Voice Memo is fine), export it as `.m4a`, save it as
`public/intro/<code>.m4a` (`en`, `fr`, `es`, `zh`), and uncomment that
language's line in `lib/intros.ts`.

## English (`en.m4a`)

Hi, I'm Elliot. I build AI for people who can't afford it to be wrong:
students without a tutor at home, families sorting out an estate, and the
teachers who check its work. On this site you'll find what I've built, what
I've written, and how I check it, including the parts that didn't work. If
something here is useful, or you'd like to argue with it, my email is at the
bottom of every page.
